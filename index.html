The pitch

Sol has the theme of scientific accuracy — it's not trying to be a real simulation. You build a spaceship out of shaped blocks on a grid, run its cables and power by hand, and fly it through the actual solar system: every planet and moon at true scale, on its real orbit. Ships take structural damage and break apart along their own geometry. Land on any rocky world and walk it under its own gravity. Long term, you found a corporation, mine and trade across the system, and eventually terraform.

Realism is there because it makes the world make sense, not because accuracy is the goal by itself. Where the real math would make you wait or do arithmetic by hand, the numbers get bent on purpose: sci-fi reactors and thrust, a jump drive for distance, an autopilot that'll fly the orbital mechanics for you if you'd rather not. The one thing that doesn't bend is time. No time warp, ever.

Design pillars
01

Build it, then live in it

The ship is a place, not a stat block. You walk its corridors, run its cables, and watch its screens.

02

The real solar system

Actual bodies, actual distances, actual orbits. Real elevation data where it exists. Millions of asteroids that are always in the same place.

03

Nobody waits, nobody does arithmetic

Sci-fi power and thrust, a smart autopilot, a jump drive for distance. No time warp — ever.

04

Consequences are physical

Damage is structural, power is a budget, mass is real. A ship that cannot lift itself does not lift.

Technical
Engine
Godot 4.5, GDScript, static typing throughout.
Scale
Double-precision logical positions, a floating origin, and body-relative frames — the whole solar system fits, and the ground under your feet is still exact.
Terrain
Cube-sphere quadtree with geomorphing; real elevation data (LOLA, MESSENGER) where it exists, procedural detail on top.
Quality
A headless test harness runs on every change; every new check is mutation-tested before it's trusted.
Data
Real astronomical data throughout, credited by source — NASA, USGS, Solar System Scope (CC BY), Poly Haven (CC0).
Tunability
Every balance value is an exported dial. Balance is done later, by turning them — not by rewriting.
